# Pebble Crew 0.7.22 preview 61

This brings the public source up to the privately installed 0.7.22 preview 60 and removes video calls.

- Video calls are gone. Settings and the Voice tab no longer offer Atmee keys or video avatars, and saved video calls show as "Call ended". Saved keys, avatars and call records stay on the owner host.
- Includes everything from preview 60: Grok and ElevenLabs voice through the Pebble's own Bot Chat, dictation in the composer, message alerts on each device, unread markers and sidebar previews, conversation memory, the photo gallery with smaller photo copies, document diagrams and full-page documents, and a stricter owner-host content security policy.
- Includes 0.7.22 preview 1's fixes: recent chat after tool-heavy history, phone focus, pinned role labels, image download contrast, touch-and-hold sidebar options, the `/model` picker and Markdown on both sides of chat.

Use the [combined plugin guide](../AGENT_INSTALL.md) for installation and updates.
